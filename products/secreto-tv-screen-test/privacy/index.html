Privacy Policy

Secreto TV Screen Test

This Privacy Policy explains how Secreto TV Screen Test handles information when you use the application.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Data Collection

Secreto TV Screen Test does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information through its core application functionality.

Analytics and Advertising

The current version of Secreto TV Screen Test does not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

Display and Device Information

The application may access technical display or device information made available by the Android operating system in order to provide its screen-testing and display-information features. This information is used locally by the application to provide these features.

Internet and External Services

The current application version does not require Internet access for its core screen-testing functionality. Links to official SecretoTools web pages, such as support or privacy information, may open outside the application using compatible software available on the device.

Data Sharing

Secreto TV Screen Test does not sell personal information. The application's core screen-testing functionality does not transmit collected personal information to SecretoTools servers.

Children's Privacy

Secreto TV Screen Test is a general-purpose display utility and is not specifically directed at children.

Policy updates

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on the official SecretoTools website.

Contact

Questions about privacy?

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Secreto TV Screen Test, contact SecretoTools at support@secretotools.com.
