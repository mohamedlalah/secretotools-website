Frequently asked questions
What is Secreto TV Screen Test?

A TV-first utility for inspecting Android TV and Google TV displays with screen tests, visual patterns and display information.

Does the app require an account?

No. The current version does not require an account.

Does the app contain advertising or analytics?

The current version does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Do I need an Internet connection?

The current version does not require Internet access for its core screen-testing functionality.

Does the app automatically calibrate my TV?

No. The tests are intended for visual inspection and do not replace professional calibration equipment.
