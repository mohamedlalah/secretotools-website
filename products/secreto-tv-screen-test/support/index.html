Official Support

Secreto TV Screen Test — Support

Need help with Secreto TV Screen Test? Find answers to common questions or contact the official SecretoTools support address.

Before contacting support

A few details can help us troubleshoot faster.

  • Make sure you are using a compatible Android TV or Google TV device.
  • Use the latest official version of Secreto TV Screen Test available to you.
  • Note your TV or streaming-device model.
  • Include your Android version and a short description of the issue.

Device differences

Displayed information and test behavior may vary depending on the television, streaming device, Android version, manufacturer firmware and display configuration.

Frequently asked questions
Does Secreto TV Screen Test modify my television settings?

The screen tests are designed for visual inspection. They do not automatically calibrate your television or replace professional calibration equipment.

Why can results differ between devices?

Android TV devices, televisions and streaming boxes can report or process display information differently depending on their hardware, Android version and manufacturer firmware.

Do I need an Internet connection?

The current version does not require Internet access for its core screen-testing functionality.

Does the app require an account?

No. The current version does not require an account.

Does the app contain advertising or analytics?

The current version does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Contact Support

Still need help?

Contact SecretoTools at support@secretotools.com. When reporting a problem, include your device model, Android version and a short description of what happened.
