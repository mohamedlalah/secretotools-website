Secreto TV Launcher — Privacy Policy

This policy describes the app's main data flows. Features and third-party services may change between releases.

Local preferences

Favorites, hidden apps, appearance settings and other preferences are stored on the device. Android backup behavior depends on device configuration.

Network requests

When enabled, weather may send coordinates or location-related information and your IP address to Open-Meteo. Prayer-time features may contact Aladhan. Update checks contact GitHub. These providers receive technical request information under their own policies.

Accessibility

The optional Home redirect accessibility service is used to help return to the launcher. It must be enabled by the user in Android settings. It is not intended to collect typed text.

Accounts and monetization

The launcher does not require an account, paid subscription or advertising SDK. An optional GitHub Sponsors link may be shown.

Choices and contact

You can disable optional features and manage permissions in device settings. For questions, contact support@secretotools.com.
