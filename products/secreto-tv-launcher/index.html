Secreto TV Launcher

A customizable home screen built for Android TV and Google TV remotes.

Official releasesPrivacy policy

Features

Your apps, your way

Arrange favorites, search installed apps and hide apps you do not want on the home screen.

TV-first navigation

Navigate with the D-pad and adjust the look with Ocean Blue styling and backgrounds.

Optional information

Weather and prayer-time features are configurable; weather availability is subject to provider terms.

Updates and privacy

Built-in GitHub release update checks, local preferences and an optional accessibility-based Home redirect.

Release and compatibility

The next public release is being prepared. The currently tested candidate targets Android TV / Google TV devices running Android 8.0 (API 26) or later. Device behavior varies; the app is not a system-level replacement on every device.

Check GitHub Releases for published APKs. Do not assume the upcoming version is available yet.
