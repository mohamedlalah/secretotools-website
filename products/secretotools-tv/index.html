Frequently asked questions
What is SecretoTools TV?

A diagnostic utility designed specifically for Android TV and Google TV devices.

Is the app free?

Yes. The current version is available free from the official Google Play listing.

Does it collect personal data?

The app displays technical information locally, requires no account and contains no advertising SDK.

Why are some installed apps not displayed?

Android limits app discovery. SecretoTools TV can only show applications visible under Android package visibility and security rules.

Will every result be identical on all TV boxes?

No. Results depend on the Android version, firmware and information reported by each manufacturer.
