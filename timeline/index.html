Status guide

What each status means.

Clear labels prevent prototypes, experiments and published products from being confused with one another.

Released

Publicly available through an official distribution channel and supported by product documentation.

Version 1.0

Actively built and tested, but not presented as a finished public release.

Exploration

An idea being evaluated for usefulness, feasibility, privacy and store-policy compliance.
