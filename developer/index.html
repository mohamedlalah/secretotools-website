Engineering principles

The standards behind every product.

Features are valuable only when they remain understandable, stable and respectful of the person using them.

01

Privacy first

We avoid unnecessary collection and keep processing on the device or local network whenever possible.

02

Performance matters

Interfaces and background work are designed to remain responsive across varied television hardware.

03

TV-first experience

Remote navigation, focus states and distance readability are treated as core requirements.

04

Quality before features

A focused set of dependable capabilities is better than a long list of unfinished ideas.

05

Long-term support

Released products are documented, monitored and improved instead of being abandoned.

06

User-centered design

Real feedback and real usage problems guide decisions throughout the product lifecycle.

Development workflow

From a real problem to a supported release.

Every stage must produce evidence before a product advances.

  1. 1

    Research

    Identify a genuine need among Android TV and Google TV users.

  2. 2

    Prototype

    Build the smallest reliable version of the core workflow.

  3. 3

    Internal testing

    Validate behavior, navigation, storage, network and compatibility.

  4. 4

    Closed testing

    Collect structured feedback from real users and varied hardware.

  5. 5

    Google Play review

    Confirm policy compliance, release quality and documentation.

  6. 6

    Public release

    Publish only after the product and support materials are ready.

  7. 7

    Continuous improvement

    Use feedback and diagnostics to prioritize meaningful updates.

Quality assurance

Tested beyond a single emulator.

Android TV hardware differs by manufacturer, Android version, memory, storage and certification. Testing combines physical devices, emulation and structured user feedback.

  • Physical Android TV and Google TV devices
  • Xiaomi devices and TV boxes from varied manufacturers
  • Remote-control navigation and focus behavior
  • Storage, network and permission edge cases
  • Emulator checks and closed testing
  • Performance analysis, UI validation and policy review
