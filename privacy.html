SecretoTools TV Privacy Policy

SecretoTools TV is designed to work without account creation and without collecting personal information. The app may read technical device information locally to show diagnostics such as storage, network status, codecs and supported applications. This information is used on the device to help the user understand compatibility and device status. Official Google Play privacy disclosures will be kept aligned with this page.

Key points

  • Static website optimized for speed and security.
  • No unnecessary tracking scripts are included in this release.
  • Official links should always use secretotools.com.
