Building SecretoTools TV for Android TV

How SecretoTools TV was shaped around the realities of television hardware, remote navigation and clear diagnostics.

By Mohamed LALAH2026-07-308 min read

SecretoTools is developed as a practical software project, with decisions tested against real television devices and real user needs. This article documents the principles behind that work.

Start with the television, not the phone

Television software must be readable from a distance and controllable with a directional remote. SecretoTools TV therefore treats focus visibility, large targets and predictable navigation as core engineering requirements.

Turn technical data into useful answers

Raw system values are rarely enough. Device, storage, network and media information must be translated into clear labels that help users understand what their television can actually do.

Build for imperfect hardware

Android TV devices vary widely in memory, firmware quality and manufacturer customization. The app is designed to remain useful on older and lower-powered hardware without hiding important limitations.
