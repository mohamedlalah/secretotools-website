Standard

Publication overview

SecretoTools is an independent software initiative focused on creating useful, privacy-conscious applications for Android TV and Google TV. Its products are designed around real living-room problems, remote-control usability and compatibility across varied television hardware. Development combines modern Android technologies, physical-device validation, closed testing and transparent product documentation. The ecosystem currently includes SecretoTools TV, a diagnostic toolkit, and Secreto Hub, a secure local-network file-transfer utility for Android TV and Google TV.

Full

Extended company profile

SecretoTools is an independent software initiative founded by Mohamed LALAH in Morocco. The project develops focused utilities that improve the Android TV and Google TV experience without relying on unnecessary data collection or complicated workflows. Each product begins with a real problem observed among television users and is then shaped through research, prototyping, physical-device testing and structured feedback.

SecretoTools TV provides clear information about device hardware, storage, network status, codecs, installed supported applications and other television capabilities. Secreto Hub extends the ecosystem with a local-network workflow for transferring files from a phone or computer browser directly to a television without requiring a companion mobile application or an external cloud server.

The initiative prioritizes television-first interfaces, dependable remote navigation, readable layouts, privacy-conscious architecture and long-term product improvement. SecretoTools products are documented through the official website and prepared with Google Play requirements, user safety and platform compatibility in mind.

Press FAQ

Can I use the SecretoTools logo in an article or video?

Yes. Official assets may be used for factual editorial coverage, reviews and references to SecretoTools, provided the logo is not altered and the use does not imply endorsement.

Can I review SecretoTools products?

Yes. Independent reviews are welcome. Clearly distinguish verified product facts from personal opinions and use official product links where possible.

Basic editorial cropping or resizing is acceptable. Do not alter the interface, remove important context or create a misleading representation of the product.

Where can I verify the latest information?

The official product pages, timeline, developer center and this press kit are the authoritative sources. Contact support@secretotools.com when confirmation is required.
