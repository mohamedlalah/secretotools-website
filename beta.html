The correct way to join

Follow these steps to avoid eligibility or compatibility messages.

1

Complete the access request form

Register your Gmail address and device type through the sign-up form.

2

Wait for your account to be added

Email addresses are added to the Google Play Console tester list in batches.

3

Open Google Play

After your account is added, use the Google Play button on this page.

4

Select Become a Tester

Officially join the testing program from the Google Play page.

5

Open the store on your TV

Open Google Play on Android TV or Google TV using the same Gmail account.

6

Install and test the app

Find SecretoTools TV, install it and try the available features.

7

Send your feedback

After testing, use the feedback form to report problems or suggestions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I install the app directly from Google Play?

No. Your email address must first be added to the tester list in Google Play Console.

What should I do if the app is shown as incompatible?

Confirm that you are using Android TV, Google TV or an Android TV Box, and that you signed in with the same Gmail account that was added.

Does the app work on phones?

No. The application is intended only for Android TV and Google TV devices.

How do I report a problem or suggestion?

Use the feedback button on this page after testing the application.
